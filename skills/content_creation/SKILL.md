# Content Creation Skill

**Skill ID**: `content_creation`  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Category**: Writing & Creative

## Description

Professional content creation across formats: blog posts, social media, emails, ad copy, and SEO optimization.

## Capabilities

### Writing Formats
- Blog posts and articles
- Social media posts (all platforms)
- Email sequences
- Ad copy and CTAs
- Product descriptions
- Video scripts

### Optimization
- SEO keyword integration
- Tone and voice matching
- Platform-specific formatting
- A/B test variations

### Multi-Platform
- LinkedIn professional content
- Twitter/X threads
- Instagram captions
- Facebook posts
- Email marketing
- Landing pages

## Tools Required

- `generate_blog_post` - Long-form content
- `generate_social_media_posts` - Social content
- `generate_email_campaign` - Email sequences
- `generate_ad_copy` - Advertising copy
- `generate_product_description` - E-commerce copy
- `generate_seo_content` - SEO-optimized content
- `improve_text` - Content enhancement

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Blog Content Series
```
Task: "Write a 5-part blog series on sustainable fashion"

Workflow:
1. Research topic and create outline
2. Write each article (800-1200 words)
3. Optimize for SEO
4. Generate social posts to promote
5. Create email to announce series
```

### Example 2: Product Launch Content
```
Task: "Create all content for new product launch"

Workflow:
1. Write product description
2. Create 10 social media posts
3. Design email sequence (5 emails)
4. Generate 5 ad copy variations
5. Write press release
```

## Success Criteria

- ✅ Content matches brand voice
- ✅ Proper grammar and spelling
- ✅ SEO keywords integrated naturally
- ✅ Appropriate length for platform
- ✅ Clear CTAs included

## Safety & Validation

- No plagiarism - all original content
- Fact-check claims and statistics
- Avoid controversial topics unless specified
- Maintain professional tone
- Follow platform guidelines

## Performance Metrics

- Quality score: > 0.85
- SEO score: > 80/100
- Engagement prediction: > 0.7
- Time per piece: < 3 minutes
