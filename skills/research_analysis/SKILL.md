# Research & Analysis Skill

**Skill ID**: `research_analysis`  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Category**: Research & Data

## Description

Comprehensive research and competitive analysis capabilities. Gather insights, analyze trends, and synthesize information from web sources.

## Capabilities

### Market Research
- Trend analysis and identification
- Competitor analysis
- Market sizing and opportunity assessment
- Customer sentiment analysis

### Web Research
- Search and aggregate information
- Extract data from websites
- Summarize long-form content
- Verify facts and sources

### Data Analysis
- Process and analyze datasets
- Generate insights from data
- Create reports and visualizations
- Calculate statistics and trends

## Tools Required

- `search_web` - Web search via Serper
- `browse_url` - Content extraction
- `research_topic` - Deep research
- `analyze_competitor` - Competitive intel
- `get_trending_topics` - Trend discovery

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Market Analysis
```
Task: "Research sustainable fashion trends for 2026"

Workflow:
1. Search for recent articles and reports
2. Identify top 10 trends
3. Analyze consumer sentiment
4. Compile competitive landscape
5. Generate trend report
```

### Example 2: Competitor Analysis
```
Task: "Analyze top 5 competitors in eco-friendly apparel"

Workflow:
1. Identify key competitors
2. Analyze their product offerings
3. Review pricing strategies
4. Evaluate marketing approaches
5. Find gaps and opportunities
```

## Success Criteria

- ✅ Minimum 10 credible sources
- ✅ Data is current (< 6 months old)
- ✅ Multiple perspectives included
- ✅ Actionable insights generated
- ✅ Sources properly cited

## Safety & Validation

- Verify source credibility
- Cross-reference facts
- Avoid biased sources
- Respect robots.txt and rate limits
- Don't scrape personal data

## Performance Metrics

- Research depth: > 10 sources
- Time to insight: < 5 minutes
- Accuracy: > 95%
- Actionability score: > 0.8
