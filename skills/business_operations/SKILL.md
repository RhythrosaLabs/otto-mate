# Business Operations Skill

**Skill ID**: `business_operations`  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Category**: Business & E-commerce

## Description

Autonomous business operations including product creation, marketing campaigns, e-commerce management, and performance tracking.

## Capabilities

### Product Creation
- Generate product designs using AI image generation
- Create print-on-demand products (Printify)
- Design mockups and lifestyle scenes
- Write SEO-optimized product descriptions

### E-Commerce Management
- Create and update Shopify products
- Manage inventory and pricing
- Publish products to online stores
- Track product performance

### Marketing & Content
- Generate ad copy and marketing materials
- Create social media content
- Design email campaigns
- Write blog posts and SEO content

### Analytics & Optimization
- Track business KPIs
- Analyze product performance
- Calculate ROI on campaigns
- Generate business reports

## Tools Required

- `generate_image` - AI image generation
- `printify_create_product` - POD product creation
- `printify_publish_product` - Product publishing
- `shopify_create_product` - E-commerce listing
- `generate_product_description` - SEO content
- `generate_ad_copy` - Marketing copy
- `generate_social_media_posts` - Social content
- `research_topic` - Market research

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Launch Product Line
```
Task: "Create 3 t-shirt designs with mountain themes and publish to Shopify"

Workflow:
1. Research mountain design trends
2. Generate 3 unique designs
3. Create Printify products for each
4. Write product descriptions
5. Publish to Shopify
6. Generate marketing content
```

### Example 2: Marketing Campaign
```
Task: "Create a week-long social media campaign for our new product"

Workflow:
1. Analyze product and target audience
2. Generate campaign strategy
3. Create 7 posts with images
4. Write ad copy variations
5. Schedule content calendar
```

## Success Criteria

### Product Creation
- ✅ Design meets brand guidelines
- ✅ Product created in Printify
- ✅ Listed on Shopify with proper details
- ✅ Competitive pricing set
- ✅ Marketing materials generated

### Marketing Campaign
- ✅ 5+ content pieces created
- ✅ Multi-platform coverage
- ✅ Brand consistency maintained
- ✅ CTAs included
- ✅ Performance tracking setup

## Safety & Validation

### Pre-Execution Checks
- Validate brand guidelines
- Check budget constraints
- Verify product details
- Confirm pricing strategy

### Post-Execution Verification
- Review generated content quality
- Verify product listings are live
- Check image quality and format
- Validate SEO optimization

## Business Rules

1. **Pricing**: Must maintain profit margins > 30%
2. **Branding**: All content must align with brand voice
3. **Quality**: Generated images must be high-resolution
4. **Compliance**: Follow platform guidelines (Printify, Shopify)
5. **Budget**: Track costs for AI generation and services

## Performance Metrics

- Time to market: < 30 minutes per product
- Content quality score: > 0.8
- SEO optimization: > 85/100
- Success rate: > 90%
