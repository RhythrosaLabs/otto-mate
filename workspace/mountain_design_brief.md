# Minimalist Mountain Landscape Design

## Design Concept
A clean, modern mountain landscape featuring:
- Simple geometric mountain silhouettes
- Limited color palette (2-3 colors)
- Emphasis on negative space
- Modern flat design aesthetic

## Applications
- Wall art and prints
- Digital backgrounds
- Product design elements
- Branding materials

## Style Notes
- Vector-style illustration
- High contrast elements
- Professional quality
- Scalable design
