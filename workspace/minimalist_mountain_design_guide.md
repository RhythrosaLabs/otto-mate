# Minimalist Mountain Landscape Design

## Design Specifications
- Style: Minimalist geometric illustration
- Color Palette: Limited 2-3 colors
- Composition: Simple mountain silhouettes
- Aesthetic: Modern, clean, Scandinavian-inspired

## Usage Recommendations
- T-shirt and apparel designs
- Wall art and home decor
- Website headers and backgrounds
- Business branding elements
- Social media graphics

## Design Principles Applied
- Negative space utilization
- Geometric simplification
- High contrast elements
- Scalable vector-style approach
- Timeless aesthetic appeal

## File Formats
- High resolution for print use
- Suitable for digital applications
